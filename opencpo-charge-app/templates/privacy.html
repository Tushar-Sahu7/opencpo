Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data is processed when using this charging service and how long it is retained.

1. Who are we?

The operator of this charging network is responsible for the processing of your personal data when you use this charging service. Contact details are provided in your service agreement.

2. What data do we collect?

  • Contact details: phone number and email address (for verification and receipts)
  • Charging data: location, timestamp, energy consumed (kWh), cost
  • Payment data: payment status (no bank details are stored by us)
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type (required for service operation)

3. Why do we process your data?

  • Delivering the charging service (contract performance)
  • Sending a receipt by email
  • SMS verification (OTP)
  • Invoicing and accounting (legal obligation)
  • Service improvement

4. Cookies and local storage

We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. We do use:

  • localStorage: for session information and your consent preference
  • Functional cookies: required for payment service operation

5. Retention periods

  • Charging session data: 7 years (statutory accounting requirement)
  • Contact details: while you have an active account, plus up to 1 year after last session
  • Payment data: 7 years (statutory accounting requirement)

6. Sharing with third parties

We only share your data with:

  • Payment processor: for payment processing
  • SMS provider: for SMS verification
  • Email provider: for sending receipts

We do not sell your data to third parties.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data
  • Restrict processing
  • Data portability
  • Object to processing

Contact the service operator to exercise your rights.

8. Complaints

You may file a complaint with your national data protection authority.

9. Contact

Contact your service operator for privacy-related inquiries.
