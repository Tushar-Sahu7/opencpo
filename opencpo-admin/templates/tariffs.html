Tariffs

Tariffs control how much drivers pay when they charge. Each tariff is a pricing rule you assign to one or more chargers. Drivers see the price before they start charging.

Energy Rate (€/kWh)The main charge — drivers pay this per kilowatt-hour of electricity delivered. Most tariffs only need this.
Time Rate (€/min)Optional per-minute fee while charging. Useful for busy locations where you want to encourage faster turnover.
Idle Rate (€/min)Fee that kicks in after charging is complete but the car is still plugged in. Encourages drivers to free up the charger promptly.
Flat Fee (€)A fixed connection fee charged once per session, regardless of energy or time. Can be used to cover infrastructure costs.
